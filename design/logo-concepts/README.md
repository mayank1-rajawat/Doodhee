# Doodhee logo concepts

These identity directions were created from Doodhee's product vision and the Doodhee Fresh color system.

## Foundation exploration

1. **Doorstep Drop** — milk, home delivery, and the doorstep route in a D-shaped mark.
2. **Verified Milkman** — a local milk can, service-area pin, and verified-seller check.
3. **Local Trust Loop** — buyer and seller connected around a shared milk drop.
4. **Morning Route** — the daily early-morning delivery rhythm and route.
5. **Honest Daily Ledger** — milk quantity, delivery record, and trustworthy billing.
6. **Doodhee Rhythm** — a wordmark-led system built around recurring milk delivery.

## Contemporary exploration — round two

7. **Precision D** — screen-first hyper-minimal monogram with a drop-shaped counter.
8. **Milk Pebble** — tactile soft-3D app icon for a friendly premium mobile presence.
9. **Human Loop** — organic buyer–seller connection and recurring daily service.
10. **Indic Rhythm** — contemporary Latin lettering informed by North Indian typographic rhythm.
11. **5 AM Milk Token** — neo-retro neighborhood warmth centered on the morning delivery ritual.
12. **Editorial Trust** — mature quality-and-transparency positioning with a soft serif wordmark.
13. **Daily System** — modular identity designed to extend into UI status and motion components.
14. **One Continuous Journey** — motion-ready order-to-delivery-to-record ribbon mark.

The PNGs are high-resolution exploration assets with transparent backgrounds. After a direction is selected, it should be refined into production SVG, app-icon, monochrome, small-size, and light/dark lockups.
