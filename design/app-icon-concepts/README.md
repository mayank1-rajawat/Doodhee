# Doodhee app-icon concepts

Purpose-built launcher icons for the Doodhee Android and iOS apps. Each concept combines cow milk, buffalo milk, and the Doodhee marketplace story while retaining a bold small-screen silhouette.

- **A — Twin Origins:** cow and buffalo profiles meet around a milk drop inside a D.
- **B — Cow + Buffalo Unity:** one unified dairy face with distinct cow and buffalo features.
- **C — Dairy D:** a D monogram built from a cow ear, buffalo horn, and milk drop.
- **D — Milk Source:** an ultra-simple cow-ear, buffalo-horn, and milk-drop crest.
- **E — Local Milk Can:** neighborhood milk can fused with cow, buffalo, and D symbolism.
- **F — From Herd to Home:** cow and buffalo forms lead into a household doorway.

These are high-resolution concept PNGs. The selected direction should be redrawn as clean vector geometry, then exported as Android adaptive foreground/background layers and iOS icon sizes.

## Milkman-focused exploration

This round removes the standalone liquid drop so the icon cannot be mistaken for a water app.

- **G — Local Milkman:** seller carrying separate cow- and buffalo-milk cans.
- **H — Morning Milk Route:** early-morning scooter delivery with two dairy-source cans.
- **I — Milkman at the Door:** local seller and traditional can delivering to a household.
- **J — Milk Can Hero:** one compact mark merging the seller and the milk can.
- **K — Verified Milk Seller:** trusted local seller holding a verified dairy can.
- **L — Source, Seller, Service:** cow, buffalo, milkman, and milk can in one emblem.

## Vision-document shortlist

These concepts were derived from the complete Vision & Scope document rather than from dairy imagery alone.

- **M — Verified Milk Can:** local loose milk, cow/buffalo source cues, and approved quality.
- **N — Seller to Home:** Doodhee as the connection between an independent milkman and a household.
- **O — Two Sources, One Can:** cow and buffalo milk choice with one verified marketplace.
- **P — Honest Measure:** exact quantity, transparent records, billing, and measured trust.
